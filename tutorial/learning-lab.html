04 / THINK LIKE A SCIENTIST

Can we trust the conclusion?

A quick question

A dataset contains 10,000 cells from one donor. How many independent donor replicates does it have?

Choose an answer.

Before making a claim

Choose donor-disjoint development and test sets, check batch and state definitions, compare appropriate baselines and matched control edits, and summarize effects across donors.

Prioritize candidates for independent experimental testing. A shift in an embedding is a model hypothesis, not measured treatment response.
